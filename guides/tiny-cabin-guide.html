Tiny Off-Grid Cabin: Site, Permission and Project Planning

Prepare a cabin project brief covering intended use, site investigation, local permissions, structural design, building assemblies, services and costs.

Define the Building and Its Use

Write a brief covering occupants, sleeping and cooking, seasonal or full-time use, access, water, sanitation, heating and electricity. Distinguish a dwelling from a storage shed or workshop before choosing plans.

Make a room and equipment layout showing usable space, doors, access and maintenance routes. Resolve loft access and escape provisions through the design rather than treating a small sleeping platform as a free addition.

Check the Site Before Choosing a Foundation

Record boundaries, access, slopes, surface water, ground conditions, existing services and the proposed water and wastewater locations. Investigate local hazards and restrictions before clearing or excavating.

Welsh Government foundation guidance explains that soil bearing capacity, moisture-related movement, nearby structures and other ground conditions influence foundation design. Its guidance concerns extensions in Wales; the general lesson is to investigate the actual site rather than copy a depth or foundation size.

Confirm the Dwelling Approval Route

Kirkland, Washington lists detached structures under 200 square feet used for anything other than storage as requiring a permit. Its limited storage-shed exemption has conditions and does not waive other rules.

Unincorporated King County also describes its small-shed exemption as an accessory storage use, with critical-area qualifications. These are local examples, not rules for every rural property.

Ask the responsible authority about the proposed use, zoning, setbacks, hazards, services, drawings, inspections and permission to occupy. Get the requirements for the actual property before ordering a kit or beginning work.

Compare Foundation Proposals

Have the building loads and ground information considered together. The Welsh guidance explains why foundation type and dimensions vary between projects. Compare complete proposals with their assumptions, investigation and inspection requirements.

Concrete Piers

Resolve bearing, uplift, lateral resistance, connections and the floor spans as one design. Do not specify a pier diameter and count from cabin floor area alone.

Screw or Helical Piles

Confirm suitability for the actual ground, installation access, verified capacity, corrosion provisions and connection details. A difficult site is not proof that one pile type will work.

Pads or Skids

Confirm whether the proposed support arrangement is appropriate and accepted for the intended building use. Calling a cabin temporary or movable does not establish an exemption.

Continuous Foundations or Slabs

Include ground preparation, drainage, moisture control, insulation and services in the proposal. Confirm what must be inspected before it is concealed.

Specify a Complete Structural System

Use a documented design addressing the roof, walls, floors, foundations, connections and openings together. Include the actual wind and snow conditions, roof covering, lofts, tanks and other equipment loads.

A stock timber size, stud spacing or roof pitch is not a complete design. Coordinate service routes before drilling or cutting structural members. Have proposed changes reviewed through the agreed design process.

Design Insulation, Moisture Control and Ventilation Together

The US Department of Energy's building-science overview explains that vapour control depends on climate and cladding, and that an unsuitable layer can prevent an assembly from drying. Do not apply one internal plastic-sheet rule to every cabin.

Specify the complete wall, roof and floor assemblies with weather protection, drainage, air sealing, insulation and ventilation. Check how windows, doors and penetrations connect to those layers. A cavity insulation rating alone does not describe the performance of the finished building.

Common Questions

Does a small footprint make a cabin exempt from permits?

No universal size exemption applies to a dwelling. Confirm the proposed use and the requirements for the property with the responsible authorities.

Can I choose foundations from a generic pier-size chart?

Have the foundation selected for the building loads, ground conditions and local requirements. A generic diameter, count or frost-depth assumption is not a site design.

Does off-grid living remove wastewater requirements?

Confirm the approved water and wastewater arrangements for the property. A composting toilet or greywater proposal does not by itself resolve those requirements.

How much will the cabin cost?

Obtain a complete local scope and quotations, including site work, design, permissions, shell, services, access and inspections. This guide does not establish a build price or completion time.
