Off-Grid Gear Buying Guides

Buying guides for common off-grid equipment, built from published specifications, manuals, warranty terms and independent certifications. We do not run hands-on tests. No paid placements; affiliate links are clearly marked.

By The Off-Grid Workshop

About these guides

Earlier versions of these pages reported test results and scores that we could not substantiate. Scores have been removed and the pages are being revised one by one; until a page carries a revision note, disregard any test language on it. Verify the exact model against current manufacturer documentation, and size equipment from your own power audit. How we research.
