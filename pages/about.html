About The Off-Grid Workshop

The Off-Grid Workshop publishes practical, research-based guides for people planning a more self-reliant home: off-grid solar power, water supply and storage, food growing and preservation, and small buildings. We focus on the planning maths and the checks that stop expensive mistakes, rather than lifestyle content.

What we do

  • Explain the method. Guides show the formulas behind sizing and planning decisions, with worked examples labelled as illustrations, so you can repeat the calculation with your own numbers.
  • Cite primary sources. Technical and safety guidance is checked against manufacturer documentation, standards bodies, government agencies and university extension services. Source lists appear on the page.
  • Separate facts from judgement. Where we give an opinion about a product or approach, we say what it is based on.
  • Build planning tools. Free calculators on the site, and paid workbooks in the shop for readers who want a complete, reusable planning file.

What we do not claim

We are not a test laboratory and we do not publish product measurements unless a page states exactly how they were obtained. Product pages are buying guides built from published specifications, manuals, warranty terms and independent certifications. We do not invent reviews, testimonials, ratings or personal experience.

Our guides are general information, not a design or installation service. Electrical, structural, drinking-water, gas, wood-burning and food-preservation work can injure people or damage property. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and your local codes, and use a qualified, licensed professional where the law or the risk requires one.

A correction to earlier versions of this site

Earlier versions of this site were written in the voice of a named homesteader and described hands-on test results, star ratings and a personal off-grid property. We could not substantiate those claims, so in November 2026 we removed the persona and are revising every affected page. If you spot a claim we have missed, please tell us and we will correct it.

How we make money

The site is funded by three things, all disclosed where they appear:

  • Affiliate links. Some product links (for example, to Amazon) earn us a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. A commission never changes what we write about a product. See the disclaimer.
  • Display advertising served by Google AdSense on some pages. See the privacy policy.
  • Our own digital products, such as the Off-Grid Solar Sizing Workbook.
