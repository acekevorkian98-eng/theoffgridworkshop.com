How We Research and Review

By The Off-Grid Workshop · Updated November 2026

This page explains where the information on this site comes from and what our product pages can and cannot tell you.

We do not run product tests

We do not operate a test bench, and our product pages are not hands-on reviews. They are buying guides: they summarise published specifications, manuals, warranty terms and independent certifications, then explain which buyers and uses a product suits and what to check before buying. If a page ever reports a measurement, it must say who measured it, with what equipment and under what conditions. Otherwise it is not published.

Earlier versions of this page described a home test setup and measurement protocol. We could not substantiate them, so they were withdrawn in November 2026. Pages that still need revision are being updated; any remaining test language on them should be disregarded.

Source order

  1. Codes, standards and regulators for safety-sensitive topics: for example the U.S. EPA and CDC for water, USDA and the National Center for Home Food Preservation for canning and curing, and fire-safety agencies for wood heat.
  2. University extension services and government energy agencies for planning methods and climate data, including NREL for solar resource data.
  3. Independent certification, such as NSF/ANSI listings for water treatment and UL or equivalent listings for electrical equipment, checked on the certifier’s own database where possible.
  4. Manufacturer documentation: datasheets, manuals and warranty terms, treated as the maker’s claims rather than independent verification.

Forum posts, retailer reviews and videos can point to questions worth checking, but we do not present them as evidence of performance.

Calculations and examples

Sizing guides and calculators show their formulas. Worked examples use illustrative numbers and are labelled as such. Results are planning estimates: real performance depends on the equipment, installation, weather and how the system is used.

Ratings

We no longer publish star ratings or scores, because a score implies a test we did not run. Product pages instead state who a product suits, its limitations and what to verify.

Affiliate links and advertising

Some links earn us a commission. Commissions do not decide what we cover or what we say about a product, and we link to non-affiliate sources whenever they are the better reference. Details are in the disclaimer.

Prices and availability

Prices change often. Any price on this site is an approximate guide on the date shown; check the retailer before buying.

Corrections

If you find an error, an outdated rule or a claim without support, please contact us with the page address. We correct factual errors and note significant changes on the page.
