Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains what information The Off-Grid Workshop collects, how it is used, and what third-party services operate on this site.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: New newsletter signup is currently unavailable on this site. Buttondown is the newsletter service named in our subscription information. Existing subscribers can use the unsubscribe link in a newsletter email.
  • Contact form submissions: The name, email address and message you submit through our contact form are sent to Formspree for processing. We use this information to respond to your message. Formspree also describes technical information it may collect, such as IP addresses and browser details, in its privacy policy.
  • Usage data: This site includes Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions. Analytics can collect page and session information, approximate location and device or browser information. Our site also sends an affiliate-click event with the selected link’s label. Analytics uses identifiers to distinguish users and sessions; this should not be understood as a promise of anonymous browsing. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cookies and Browser Storage

Services included on this site may use cookies and other identifiers:

  • Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish users and maintain session state. Cookie identifiers can connect activity across visits. Google notes that Analytics tags can also transmit data without cookies. See Google’s Analytics cookie documentation.
  • Google AdSense advertising code is included on this site. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.
  • Amazon Associates and other affiliate programs may set cookies when you click affiliate links, per their respective policies.

The site’s theme control stores your light/dark preference in your browser’s localStorage under ogw-theme. The theme-control code does not send this preference to a server. Clearing site storage removes the saved preference.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear site storage. Blocking cookies alone does not prevent every network request or every form of measurement. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers. This controls Analytics, rather than all advertising or third-party services.

Third-Party Services

This site relies on the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics: visitor and interaction measurement, described above.
  • Google AdSense: advertising code (publisher ID: ca-pub-9986267429592601). Google describes its data use in the partner-sites privacy information.
  • Google Fonts: the page requests stylesheets and typefaces from Google’s servers. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Buttondown: the newsletter service named in our subscription information. New signup is currently unavailable.
  • Formspree: contact form processing, subject to the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Associates and other linked retailers: affiliate links lead to external sites with their own privacy practices. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • GitHub Pages: site hosting. GitHub states that Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

How We Use Your Information

  • Newsletter email addresses are used only to send the newsletter you signed up for
  • Contact form submissions are used only to respond to your message
  • Analytics data is used in aggregate to understand what content is most useful and to improve the site
  • We do not sell personal information to third parties
  • We do not share personal information with third parties except as described above (the services that process it on our behalf)

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any personal data we hold (newsletter email address or contact form submission) by contacting us directly. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights under GDPR including the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data.

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us.
